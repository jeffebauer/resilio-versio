TONE: filter before, after, or split (prototype, 3 Oct 2026)

When you turn TONE right during a ringing tail, what should thin out?
The Big Knob (TONE right of noon: the low cut, 18 dB/oct, up to 800 Hz,
with the coil bump on hits) in three places. Everything else TONE does,
and everything left of noon, is the same in all three.

0   A, before the springs (today): the next hit goes in thin; the tail already ringing hardly changes
1   B, after the springs (on the return, like Black Ark): the tail you hear thins at once
2   C, split: a gentle share before (6 dB/oct), the steeper share after (12 dB/oct); a new hit gets today's total

Rows:
  g1 tail        one snare, TONE noon, swept fully right from 0.3 s after the hit over 1.5 s, held, back to noon
  g1 tail mix04  the same at MIX 0.4 (wet under the dry)
  g2 skank       the skank loop, TONE noon -> fully right -> noon over 8 s
  g2 skank mix04 the same at MIX 0.4
  static         02 hits and 04 skank at TONE 0.7 / 0.85 / 1, no movement

Settings: 2 Springs, DECAY 0.6, TENSION noon, SPLASH 0.3, DRIVE 0.25,
WOBBLE noon (still), MIX 1 unless stated. Columns: CLEAN, KICKED.
Level matching is on (the page's toggle turns it off).
