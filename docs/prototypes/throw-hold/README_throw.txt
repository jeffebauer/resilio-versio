THROW on the gate (ADR 0039). With a gate patched, the springs' send is open only while the gate is high: dub's throw, one hit or one stab into the springs while the rest stays dry, and the tail rings on after the gate drops. Unpatched nothing changes: the send stays open until the gate's first rise. Same dub beat (75 bpm, thump on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4, offbeat stabs), DECAY 0.75, MIX 0.45. In the plugin it is the THROW switch (automatable, beside KICK). Listen for: do the thrown hits stand out like a dub throw, does anything tick when the send opens or closes, does the tail ring on naturally after the close?

A_today    the send always open (today; also what an unpatched gate gives)
B_thrown   only bar 2's last snare, bar 3's last stab and bar 4's last snare go in (gate open ~170 ms each)
