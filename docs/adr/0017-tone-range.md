# 0017 — TONE range: warm dub dark ↔ splashy, never harsh

**Status:** Accepted, 27 Sep 2026

**Decision:**
- **Fully CCW:** warm, rolled-off, dubby, with boing and drips still audible. Not muffled "through a wall".
- **Noon:** neutral.
- **Fully CW:** very bright, sizzly splash, capped so it never turns harsh or fizzy on cymbals and hats.

**Why:** Keeps TONE a hero control (SPEC §2.3.3) while keeping both ends inside the sweet spot.

**Implication:** The damping LPF cutoff has a floor (chirps must stay audible at CCW) and the CW tilt/HF boost has a ceiling. The output high-shelf and limiter (§4.8) catch harshness. Exact pivot and curve are tuned by ear against references (resolves the SPEC §10 question in principle; numbers come at M5/M8).
