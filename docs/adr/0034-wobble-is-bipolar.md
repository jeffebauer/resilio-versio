# 0034 — WOBBLE is bipolar: random wow + flutter left, sine LFO right

**Status:** Accepted, 1 Oct 2026 (owner, round 2 page: voicing D). Prototyped on `proto/bipolar-wobble` (30 Sep) and `proto/bipolar-wobble-2` (1 Oct). Supersedes ADR 0008's one-way zones.

**Context:** The owner, after the hang page and the SPLASH/DRIVE build: "I quite like the more extreme wobble, but … how random is the wobble? Is it a fixed loop, or a smooth random wave? Sometimes it sounds a bit same-same." And: "differences between wobble at 9 and 12 are very subtle." The owner's idea: make the knob "more in line with other Versio modules by making it bipolar: left of noon introduces smooth random modulation (wow and flutter), and right of noon, increase the strength of a sinusoidal LFO." On the hang page (proto/wobble-hang) they picked **B, "Springs drift together at low WOBBLE"** in every panel.

What the old knob did (ADR 0008): one knob swept depth, rate (0.12 → 1.4 Hz) and the sine's share (30 → 90 %) together. So the top end was 90 % one steady sine (the "same-same"), the random line's rate was tied to the sine's, there was no flutter, and the whole lower half spanned 0–0.75 cents per pass (held tone through the whole Tank at DECAY noon: 0.7 cents at 9 o'clock, 2.8 at noon), which is why 9 o'clock and noon sounded alike.

**Decision:**
- **Noon is still** (only the Micro-mod floor, SPEC §4.10), with a dead zone of ±3 % of travel, because a pot's physical noon doesn't read exactly 0.5.
- **Left of noon: Drift, smooth random wow + flutter**, never repeating. Wow is a random line (Catmull-Rom through seeded random points) whose every segment has its own random length, so its rate wanders (~0.2–1.5 Hz, faster as it grows). Flutter is a smaller, faster random line (~5–12 Hz). Both grow towards fully left, which is roughly as wild as the old top.
- **Right of noon: Warble, a sine LFO** getting stronger (0.6 → 1.4 Hz as it grows) up to the old top end ("clearly out of tune", which the owner keeps). Its rate drifts by ±6 % on a slow random line so it never sounds mechanical; `kLfoRateWander = 0` gives a pure sine.
- **Every step is heard:** depth follows s(a) = (e^{ka} − 1)/(e^k − 1) on each side (k = 1.6 right, 1.0 left) instead of the old k = 5.89, so each 0.1 of travel grows the heard pitch movement by ~1.5–3x and the first step off noon is already a few cents on a held tone.
- **Springs drift together at low amounts** (the hang page's B): Springs B and C take Spring A's movement scaled by their Loop length, blending to their own over 10–45 % of either side's travel. Chords fade evenly; above that the Springs move independently, as before.
- **Kept:** one generator per Spring on the Loop plus the shared Transport on the first echoes (M8), depths specified in cents per pass so WOBBLE sounds the same at every TENSION, the Gliding smoothing tier (ADR 0015), CV adding to the knob (fully left + CV sweeps random → still → LFO). The default moves from 0.2 to 0.45: a touch of shared Drift, as before about 1 cent on a held tone.
- Every number lives in `core/params/WobbleVoicing.h`; `core/dsp/Wobble.*` is the generator.

**Supersedes in ADR 0008:** the three zones along one knob (Drift 0–50 %, transition, Warble 75–100 %). Drift is now the left side and Warble the right side; "held chords stay in tune" now holds at and near noon (the default is < 3 cents), and "clearly out of tune" at both end stops. ADR 0008's Micro-mod floor under everything stays.

**Rejected:**
- *Keep one-way, just reshape the curve:* fixes "9 ≈ noon" but not "same-same" (a sine still dominates the top) and gives no choice between random and periodic.
- *A second control for the rate or the random/sine mix:* no free knob on the Versio; the bipolar layout gives both characters on one knob, like other Versio firmwares.
- *Random side as the old smoothstep random line:* its pitch stops at every random point (a regular "breathing"); Catmull-Rom keeps the slope smooth through them.
- *Flutter on the sine side too:* the owner asked for a clean sine there; flutter is part of the tape-like random side.
- *Independent Springs at every amount:* the hang (ADR 0027 "Open"): one chord note can sing on after the chord at low WOBBLE.

**Consequences:** The random side swings the Loop delay further than the old sine for the same pitch (a slow wow needs more delay swing): up to 217 samples measured, 295 reserved, so the Tank's delay pool grows 28,317 → 29,061 floats at 48 kHz (firmware pool 30,000). CPU: about +0.1 % of the Versio (measurements in `docs/m8-tuning-backlog.md` "Bipolar WOBBLE"). Tests and presets that meant "no WOBBLE" now use 0.5 (noon); ones that meant the old default use 0.45. Held-tone numbers per knob step, the M6 grid at five WOBBLE settings and the sweet-spot check: backlog "Bipolar WOBBLE". The old WOBBLE section of `SplashVoicing.h` is left in place (unused) to keep the merge with the SPLASH/DRIVE branch easy; delete it when merging.

## Round 2 (1 Oct 2026, branch `proto/bipolar-wobble-2`, still Proposed)

**Context:** The owner after round 1's page: "I'm liking the bipolar control, however I think we should slightly tone down the amount of modulation at the top end of each side. Given there's no speed control for the modulation, we need to tune it. On the smooth side, we could make it feel more like a vibrato or flutter, and the left remains for wow and flutter." No tape snags (random pitch warps: "they can feel like glitches").

**Decision (proposed, three voicings to compare by ear; `core/params/WobbleVoicing.h` "Voicings"):**
- **A** = round 1 unchanged (the page's reference).
- **B** (gentle) and **C** (more): both end stops lowered, B ×0.75 (Loop 7.5 cents per pass each side), C ×0.55 (5.5), first echo scaled the same. The middle of each side stays about where it was per pass (B left ×0.94 / right ×1.03, C ×0.80 / ×0.89): the depth curves are re-bent straighter, so each step still grows.
- **Right side is a vibrato** in B and C: 1.5 Hz just right of noon → 5.5 Hz fully right (A: 0.6 → 1.4 Hz), rate drift ±6 % kept. Most of the rate rise happens in the first half of the side (1 − (1 − a)²): a fast vibrato builds up far less in the tail than a slow sway (at 5.5 Hz a 69 ms Loop's passes are ~140° apart), so with an even rise the top steps cancelled (held tone at DECAY noon, 0.9 → 1.0: 24.9 → 22.3 cents). Now the top steps are mostly depth.
- **Flutter tremolo** (B, C; the Wear & Tear manual's idea): a small volume wobble on the Springs' wet sound following the Transport's flutter line, left side only, growing with the amount on the flutter's depth curve: **0.94 dB peak** (0.41 dB rms) fully left, 0.52 / 0.13 dB peak at 0.2 / 0.4. Exactly 1 at noon, on the right and in A.
- **Flutter speed follows the wow** (B, C): flutter rate × (1 + 0.2 × wow line), so it never settles on one speed.
- The firmware and plugin use **B** until the owner picks (`wobble::kDefaultVoicing`); the Renderer can render any of them with the hidden key `wobble_voicing` (0 / 1 / 2), so one sweep holds A / B / C.

**M6 (Ringing metric) at WOBBLE off-noon — metric fix, not a looser limit:** the flags were a measurement artefact. In the tight-tank burst tails, a Loop mode drifting with the wow by about one FFT bin (8 cents at 1.3 kHz ≈ 6 Hz ≈ one 5.9 Hz bin) made that bin's prominence **jump** 15–20 dB in one frame and then sit flat, decaying with its neighbours; the growth fit read the jump as "growth", and the "climbs in thirds" check let an early jump through. Fix in `host/common/Metrics.cpp` (the 15 dB limit is unchanged): a bin's own level follows its peak over ±2 bins (±12 Hz; `kRingingFollowBins`), and both halves of the late half must carry at least 20 % of the rise (`kRingingSteadyShare`, 1 dB slack). Before/after on voicing B, 27 tight-tank corners (06_noise_bursts, TENSION 1, DECAY ¾) × WOBBLE in 0.05 steps: 27 of 567 flagged (worst 26.6) → 1 at exactly 15.0 (KICKED, 1 Spring, TONE 1, WOBBLE 0.15: the same one-jump shape, milder; left at the limit rather than tuning the rule to it). Full M6 grid (270 non-Howl cells per WOBBLE): 0 flagged at 0 / 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 1 (worst 12.7 / 10.7 / 10.0 / 9.9 / 9.2 dB). Synthetic Ringing cases (test_metrics) keep their verdicts, resonant loops read a little higher (27.4 → 27.6, 25.2 → 27.8); new cases: plain loops with slow wow on the delay pass (≤ 10.2), a resonant one with wow is flagged. **Not re-run here:** the 39 real-tank IRs (`renders/ir_library/`, on the owner's Mac only); to re-check: `for f in renders/ir_library/*.wav; do build/rv_render --analyze "$f" | grep -o 'ringing=[^ ]*'; done` (before: 38 of 39 pass, highest 14.7; Short Spring flagged).

**Not met: the Howl check at WOBBLE 0.25** (KICKED, 1 Spring, DECAY 1, 06_noise_bursts): 4 of 54 Howl cells fail its movement clause (every 2 s window must move ≥ 0.5 % in pitch or ≥ 3 dB; worst 0.19 %), round 1: 2 cells (0.46 %). Cause: the random wow can cancel the Howl's own slow wander (AntiRes.h) for ~2 s. In B the flutter-follow changes the random path the wow takes (both share one random stream), which happens to cancel more often here (follow off: 0.37–0.88 %); A reads 0.45–0.64 % at the same cells. Unrelated to the metric change (the Howl check is separate code). Options: give the Howl hook more depth while WOBBLE's left side is up, or separate random streams (changes A's path too). Worth a listen first (KICKED, 1 Spring, DECAY max, TENSION 0, WOBBLE 9 o'clock).

**Cost:** per 32-sample tick, one extra exp on the Transport (tremolo) and one multiply (follow); per sample, the Transport's gain ramp and one multiply on the wet. Desktop, 3 Loop generators + Transport (this cloud machine, ~1.8× slower than round 1's): round-1 code 6.9 (left) / 8.2 (right) ns/sample → B 8.6 / 8.9, i.e. ≈ +1 ns on round 1's machine for the left side, ≈ 7–12 Daisy cycles ≈ **+0.1 % of the Versio** over round 1 (≈ +0.2 % over main). Memory: less delay swing than round 1 (the bound covers every voicing, A the largest): Tank 29,007 floats at 48 kHz (firmware pool 30,000). Not measured on hardware: the plugin and firmware don't build in the cloud session.

**Tests changed on purpose** (round 1's numbers the owner asked to lower): test_wobble per-pass step ≥ 1.3× → ≥ 1.15× (straighter curves; the heard step is still checked at every 0.1 in test_m7_tank); "old top end" kept for A, B within 10 % of its own end stop; "steady sine" and "no flutter on the right" windows follow the vibrato's rate; Loop-only tail end stops ≥ 25 cents → left ≥ 15, right ≥ 3 (a 5.5 Hz vibrato doesn't build up in the Loop; it is heard mostly on the first echoes); test_m7_tank end stops ≥ 25 → ≥ 15 cents at DECAY noon / max. New: toning-down ratios, tremolo size and exactly-1 elsewhere, flutter-follow mapping.
