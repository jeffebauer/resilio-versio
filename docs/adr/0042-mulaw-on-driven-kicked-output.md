# 0042 — µ-law on the DRIVEN / KICKED output

**Status:** Accepted, 4 Oct 2026 (owner: B on every panel and ATTITUDE of `renders/feat_output_mulaw/`). Amends SPEC §3 (MIX, ATTITUDE), §4.8, §7 M7. Numbers: `core/params/OutputVoicing.h`; code: `core/dsp/OutputBits.h`; method and measurements: `docs/prototypes/output-mulaw/README.md`; tests: `host/tests/test_output_bits.cpp`.

**Context:** On the echo branch's bit-depth round the owner picked 8-bit µ-law on the repeats (`echo_bits_voicing` D), then: "I really like the sound of the 8-bit µ-law, but because it's only applied to the repeats, the character is still quite subtle. Could we apply µ-law to both the dry and wet signals when the ATTITUDE switch is set to DRIVEN or KICKED?" The owner's answers:
- every SPRINGS position (position 3 is echo mode since ADR 0041: the box is after it too);
- DRIVEN = 24 kHz / 12-bit µ-law, KICKED = 24 kHz / 8-bit µ-law, CLEAN untouched (CLEAN stays a clean passthrough at MIX 0, bit for bit);
- dry and wet both: the whole output after MIX goes through the box, so MIX fully left in DRIVEN / KICKED is no longer a clean passthrough.

**Decision:**
1. **Where.** One stereo stage at the very end of the Tank: after the wet's limiter and after MIX (the dry has to be in it). The limiter keeps the wet alone under −1 dBFS; dry + wet at mid MIX can reach the box's full scale (Tank units, the Plugin's 0 dBFS) and are clipped there, like a real converter. The firmware's `kOutputTrim` (−1.17 dB and the polarity flip) comes after it.
2. **The rate.** 48 → 24 → 48 kHz through the Drive oversamplers' polyphase IIR half-band (`dsp/Oversampler.h` `Halfband`: flat to 10.6 kHz, ≥ 85 dB down from 13.4 kHz), down and up. Nothing above 12 kHz folds back into the band, so no new pitches (the owner rejected pitched aliasing in the BBD round). The top octave above ~11 kHz is gone in DRIVEN / KICKED: that is the 24 kHz sound. IIR, not the echo's 63-tap FIR: the same stop band for a fifth of the work and ~6 samples of delay instead of 62.
3. **The bits.** µ-law (µ 255) against full scale, quantised in the compressed domain, signed (0 is a code): fine steps when quiet, coarse when loud, so the grain follows the signal (SNR ~57 dB in DRIVEN, ~33 dB in KICKED). TPDF dither (±1 step) and nearest rounding down to the last step; the dither fades out between 1 and 0.5 steps of signal (1 ms envelope), and under half a step is exactly 0. **Silence in, silence out**: no signal, no dither, no hiss; the dry alone reaches exact 0 ~10 ms after the input stops, and a tail ends in soft grain, then exact silence. Two departures from the echo's version, both measured: its undithered round-toward-zero below 4 steps (needed in a feedback loop) left a pitched fizz at the end of tails here (M6 ringing_db 7.7 / 10.3 dB vs today's 0.4 / 1.8; now 0.2 / −0.6), and its 40 ms dither envelope kept a step of hiss going 0.13–0.25 s after the input stopped.
4. **Latency.** ~6 samples (0.12 ms; 3.8–6.4 samples across 100 Hz–10 kHz) in DRIVEN / KICKED only. CLEAN is never delayed, so it stays bit for bit; dry and wet go through the box together and stay aligned. The Plugin still reports latency 0.
5. **ATTITUDE flips Morph (ADR 0003).** The box's weights (CLEAN, DRIVEN, KICKED) glide linearly to the switch over 20 ms: CLEAN ↔ DRIVEN fades the box in and out against the undelayed output (for those 20 ms the two sum like a comb whose first notch is ~4 kHz; no click), DRIVEN ↔ KICKED crossfades the two depths. Back in CLEAN the box is bypassed after a 2 ms ring-out.
6. **The LEDs (ADR 0031).** The output level meters read the box's output (what leaves the Tank). Red still comes only from the limiter, which sits before the box: `Tank::limiterGain()` reads exactly as without it.
7. **Renderer.** Hidden key `output_bits_voicing`: 1 = this (default everywhere), 0 = before the box (the pre-0042 reference, and the tests' hook for reading the Tank before it). The firmware builds only the default (`RV_FIXED_VOICINGS`), so 0 costs it nothing.

**Cost.** Desktop worst case (KICKED, 3 Springs, DECAY / TONE / DRIVE / SPLASH 1, TENSION 0): +25 ns/sample (+4.5 to +6 %). Chip estimate: 12 first-order allpass sections, one `logf` and one `expf` per sample (both channels at the half rate), dither and glide: ~300–400 cycles, **+3 to +4 points**: run 16's ~68 % peak → ~71–72 %, under the 75 % target (ADR 0030); one more `expf` during a 20 ms DRIVEN ↔ KICKED crossfade. To confirm with the next M3 profile run. Flash: release 116,260 B (+3,608 over 112,652; 88.7 % of 128 KB), profile 118,208 B; about half of it is GCC now inlining `DriveOut::process` into `Tank::process`. `log`/`exp` rather than `log1p`/`expm1` (those would add 1.2 KB of newlib). Run 17 measured ~8–9 points; run 18 (`perf/run18`, bit for bit): the expansion from a table (no `expf`), steady pair loops for both channels, CLEAN running only the down filters: −1.2 points by the issue model in KICKED (more if the chip's extra cost was the box's), CLEAN ~0.3 points (firmware/README.md "Run 18 (prepared)").

**Tests adapted** (no limit loosened; what each measures is unchanged, only where it reads):
- `test_mix`, `test_main`, `plugin_host_test` MIX 0 null tests run in **CLEAN** (MIX 0 is a passthrough only there, by the owner's choice). `test_mix` gains DRIVEN / KICKED checks: MIX 0 is the dry through the box at its level (±0.1 dB on in-band noise) and not bit-identical; MIX 1 still has no dry leakage (< −80 dB).
- `plugin_host_test` MIDI Kick onset (N + 0) is read in **CLEAN** (the box's ~6 samples would move it); the Kick parity check stays in the default ATTITUDE.
- "Peak < 1" stability bars (`test_tank`, `test_spring`, `test_springs3`, `test_drive`) read the Tank **before the box** (`setOutputBitsVoicing(0)`, a test hook): the box's filters and steps can lift a limited peak past 1.0, which is the converter, not the Tank. Each also checks the shipped output (box in) finite and, where they check decay, ending no louder (`<=`: the box's tail can be exact silence, where `<` would read 0 < 0), and reports its peak and the Versio's after `kOutputTrim`.
- `test_drive` aliasing (the drive chain's oversampling) reads **before the box**; the box's own products (µ-law's slightly curved expansion runs at 24 kHz, so its highest products fold) are printed as INFO, ~30 dB under the box's own grain; `test_output_bits` checks the box for new pitches, with a positive control.
- `test_output_bits`: the default is 1, an untouched Tank = voicing 1; the LEDs' red unchanged by the box.
- After merging Throw + Hold and echo mode (ADR 0039–0041): `test_echo_mode`'s stability peaks read **before the box** (shipped output checked finite and fading, peak reported: worst 1.066, the Versio's 0.932); `test_drive`'s shipped-output check skips the Hold cells as main's own check does; `test_kick`'s "Kick < 100 Hz down ≥ 20 dB within 300 ms" (ADR 0016, the Tank's tight low end) reads **before the box**, the box's number printed (see Consequences); the LED check runs SPRINGS 3 with echo mode off (three Springs), as `test_led_meter`.

**Consequences to watch (owner, by ear):**
- KICKED is gritty on everything, dry included; at MIX fully left DRIVEN / KICKED are no longer clean (by choice). For a clean dry at MIX 0, use CLEAN.
- The top octave is gone in DRIVEN / KICKED: bright dry material (hi-hats, the air on drums) reads ~1.2 dB quieter K-weighted on 02_hits at MIX 0 (0 dB on the wet, skank, pad).
- KICKED with TONE right of noon: after a Kick, the box's grain from the still-ringing highs fills the lows, so the Kick's < 100 Hz tail falls 10–23 dB in 300 ms with the box instead of 24–35 dB before it (TONE 1 / 0.85, DECAY 1 / 0.88; at the default TONE unchanged, 38–45 dB). Heard as grain under the tail, not a boomy thump.
- Very hot moments at mid MIX can clip at the box's full scale; the shipped output can peak a little above 0 dBFS in the Plugin at extreme settings (worst in the stability grids 1.107, the Versio's 0.968 after `kOutputTrim`: under the DAC's clip in every test grid).
- On the Versio the ADC's own noise (~−90 dBFS) passes through DRIVEN as an occasional bottom step (~−100 dBFS); KICKED gates it to silence (its lowest step is ~−75 dBFS).

**Rejected:** the echo's 63-tap FIR half-bands (62 samples of delay would mean either delaying CLEAN too, so it's no longer bit for bit, or a 1.3 ms jump on every flip); the echo's toward-zero rounding and 40 ms dither envelope (point 3).
