# 0016 — Kick = tight thud + big crash

**Status:** Accepted, 27 Sep 2026

**Decision:** Kick is a short, punchy low thud followed by a big splashy crash ringing through the Springs. The low end stays tight: the thump must not linger in the tail or mud up a bassline. Tuned against the owner's recorded tank knocks (recipe take F).

**Why:** A dub backbeat hit that sits in a mix.

**Implication:** Thump decay kept short (~20–40 ms, SPEC §4.6). Low-frequency energy entering the Loop is limited (the Loop DC blocker plus a Kick-path high-pass on the part fed into the Loop). The crash is carried mostly by Clatter and Jolt. Acceptance: energy below ~100 Hz is down ≥ 20 dB within ~300 ms of a Kick (starting threshold, tune by ear).
