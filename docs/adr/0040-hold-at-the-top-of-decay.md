# 0040 — The Hold: CLEAN and DRIVEN hold at the top of DECAY, ducked

**Status:** Proposed, 4 Oct 2026 (branch `feat/throw-hold`; owner's call in the dub-lens critique, `docs/research/dub-lens-critique.md` §8: "hold at DECAY's top, CLEAN/DRIVEN, ducked; Howl stays KICKED-only"). **Round 1 picks (owner, 4 Oct, `renders/feat_throw_hold/hold/`): layer (C), and the Howl flip as today (A).** Round 2 (`renders/feat_hold_duck/`, ducking only the bed's lows) was a misreading: the owner picked round 1 (A) everywhere and corrected it ("the signal that feeds the ducking should be lowpassed so only kick/bass frequencies cause it to be ducked … the current low duck options don't sound ducked at all because there's already minimal bass presence in the feedback tail"). Round 3 (below) on `renders/feat_hold_duck3/index.html` (`docs/prototypes/hold-duck/make_page3.sh`). Amends ADR 0001 ("always fades") for CLEAN and DRIVEN above DECAY 0.9. KICKED's Howl (ADR 0002, 0018, 0019) unchanged, and so is leaving it (below).

**Context:**
- Dub techno breakdowns and siren beds want a tail that stays: turn DECAY up and the last chord sits under the next 16 or 32 bars. Today DECAY max is a 9 s T60 in every ATTITUDE, and KICKED's Howl (the only way to sustain) is noisy and crashing by design.
- A bed that sits at full level under new playing turns to mud. Dub engineers ride the return down while the band plays; the module should do that itself.
- Two ways to treat new input once the bed holds: keep it out (a frozen bed, new hits dry on top) or let it in quieter (the bed keeps growing with the music). Which one is a musical choice for the owner.

**Decision:**
- **Where:** DECAY 0.9 → 1 (the same range as KICKED's Howl zone), weighted by the CLEAN + DRIVEN Morph weight, so it fades out exactly as the Morph reaches KICKED. Below 0.9 and in KICKED everything is bit for bit as before.
- **Armed by DECAY, not by ATTITUDE** (owner, round 1: leaving the Howl must calm into the old long fade, not a held bed). The Hold arms when DECAY enters its zone outside KICKED. Any KICKED weight while DECAY is in the zone disarms it, and it stays disarmed until DECAY leaves the zone (below 0.9) and comes back. So KICKED → CLEAN / DRIVEN at DECAY max is bit for bit the Tank without the Hold (ADR 0018's long tail), and holding after that is "DECAY down a touch and back up". Why DECAY: it is the knob that asks for the Hold, so the Hold answers it; an ATTITUDE flip is the Howl's exit and keeps its meaning.
- **T60:** glides in log time (smoothstep weight) from the plain DECAY curve (6.6 s at 0.9, continuous there) to **240 s** at DECAY 1 at the Loop's design points (≈ 33 s at 0.95, 84 s at 0.97). The Spring's Loop-gain cap moves with the zone weight from 0.995 to a **peak per-trip gain of 0.998**: always under 1, so it never self-oscillates (unlike the Howl); the margin covers the peak between the design points (a dense sweep reads ≤ ~0.9993). Heard at DECAY 1: a thrown hit loses ~7 dB over 5–20 s while the damping's highs go, then ~3 dB per 10 s (≈ 190 s). The high path (fast, undispersed echoes) keeps the plain DECAY's T60, so the held wash is the Loop's dispersed sound, not a metallic comb.
- **The bed** (freeze or layer, and the ducking) eases in over the zone's first half and is fully in from DECAY 0.95.
- **Not in echo mode** (SPRINGS 3, ADR 0041): there DECAY is the echo's feedback and the Springs glide to a fixed tank (EchoVoicing.h, well below the zone), so the zone, its T60, the bed and the ducking all fade out with the echo's glide; back in positions 1-2 at DECAY max the Hold comes back in as if DECAY were turned up (re-armed, outside KICKED). Owner's pick (4 Oct): ducking B, 12 dB.
- **Ducking, round 3** (owner: duck the whole bed, but only the kick and bass should make it duck; round 1 "created not just a duck but a swell before each kick, an audible hump"). Numbers in `params/ThrowHold.h`:
  - **The whole bed dips** (a gain on the wet after the safety limiter, as round 1, so it reads the bed's own level and adds no peaks). Round 2's crossover, which ducked only the bed's lows, is gone: the springs' tail has little bass, so it hardly sounded ducked.
  - **Keyed on the input's lows:** a 4th-order low-pass at **120 Hz** on the dry input, peak follower 1 ms / 30 ms; −24 dBFS and below = no dip, −12 dBFS and above = all of it (× the bed weight). Kick and bass fundamentals (40-120 Hz) pass; a snare's body (~185 Hz) is ~15 dB down and its rattle, hats, stabs and chords further. Measured: a −6 dBFS snare + hats move the bed 0.00 dB (test_throw_hold) and 0.00 dB just after each snare on the page's snare-and-hats row, where round 1 ducked the full 12 dB. A 150 Hz key already let the snare in (0.12 dB); 100 Hz cut the kick's dip from 11.4 to 10.3 dB. A low chord stab (root 110 Hz) does key it: bass-register stabs count as bass.
  - **Depth 12 dB** (Renderer `duck_voicing` 1 = 18 dB, the page's C; 2 = none, a reference).
  - **A dip, not a hump.** Round 1's hump had two causes: the 300 ms release on a full-band, low-threshold key (still rising back into the next beat at 75 bpm), and the layer feeding the bed while it was ducked (it came back fuller each time). Now the dip falls in 3 ms, holds 20 ms after the key lets go, and is back on a 35 ms curve (within 1 dB in ~90 ms); and the layer's send dips with it, so kicks and bass don't pile into the bed.
- **Voicings** (Renderer `hold_voicing`; firmware and Plugin build the default only):
  - **"layer"** (the default since the owner's pick, 4 Oct): new input still gets in, 6 dB down (and less while the bed's lows are ducked), and builds into the bed.
  - **"freeze"**: the send closes; nothing new gets in and new hits are heard dry over the ducked bed. **An open Throw overrides the freeze** (ADR 0039): the gate is how a chord gets thrown into a frozen bed. Leaving the zone reopens the send.
- **No bed keeper.** Voicing B was measured for creep before adding one (the plan: only if > 1 dB per 10 s): a sustained pad for 60 s reads −0.07 to +0.03 dB per 10 s from 20 s on, at −3 and −12 dBFS, CLEAN and DRIVEN (`docs/prototypes/throw-hold/creep.sh`; `test_throw_hold` reports it too). The LoopSat, the Sustain trim and the limiter already hold it. None needed.

**Testable:** `test_throw_hold`: the zone's weight (CLEAN / DRIVEN = zone, KICKED 0, below 0.9 0); T60 rising and continuous (largest step ×1.055 per 0.001 of DECAY); a hit at DECAY 1, every SPRINGS, CLEAN and DRIVEN, loses 0.5–8 dB from 5 s to 20 s, never grows over 40 s, stays under the limiter; the ducking (kicks dip the whole bed ≥ 9 of the 12 dB, lows and highs alike: 11.4 / 11.3 dB; a snare + hats loop moves it ≤ 0.5 dB: 0.00; back within 0.5 dB 0.6 s after); the hump (four on the floor at 120 bpm and a one drop at 75 bpm, CLEAN / DRIVEN: the wet just before each kick at most +0.5 dB above halfway between kicks, and at most +1 dB over the same drums without ducking); the Howl flip bit for bit the Tank without the Hold, and the Hold re-arming after DECAY leaves the zone and comes back; freeze leaves the bed untouched by new input (−300 dB) and a throw gets in; layer creep reported. `test_drive`: per-trip Loop gain < 1 at every frequency including CLEAN / DRIVEN DECAY 1; the stability grid at DECAY 1 in the layer voicing never grows (judged from 3 s, after the ducking has let go of the bed); the KICKED → DRIVEN Howl exit at DECAY 1 falls as before the Hold (≥ 10 dB in 3 s, then lower). Other suites' CLEAN / DRIVEN DECAY 1 cells moved to 0.9 (the plain curve's top below the Hold): `test_antires` (evenness, pitch on held tones, tank tails, the ringing metric's control), `test_spring` (T60 at 0.9 within 15 %; stability), `test_tank` (stability; the DECAY sweep goes 0 → 0.9: entering the zone while playing ducks the wet on purpose), `test_m7_tank` (WOBBLE on held tones), `test_springs3` (SPRINGS 3 vs 2 level, width, Ringing tails).

**Consequences:**
- **Answered (owner, round 1):** flipping ATTITUDE KICKED → CLEAN / DRIVEN at DECAY max fades as before (A), not into a held bed: the arming rule above. Checked bit for bit against `main`'s Renderer on the page's Howl-flip row.
- **The hump, before / after** (`docs/prototypes/hold-duck/hump.py` on MIX 1 renders of the page's rows: the wet 60-10 ms before each kick minus halfway between kicks; positive = still rising into the kick). Four on the floor 120 bpm: round 1 −1.2 / −0.9 dB (CLEAN / DRIVEN) → round 3 −0.5 / −0.3 dB (12 dB) and −0.2 / −0.1 dB (18 dB): flat into the kick. One drop 75 bpm, lows: round 1 **+4.0 / +4.1 dB** (the hump) → +0.2 / −0.1 dB (12 dB), −6.3 dB (18 dB). The dip itself, halfway minus 30-80 ms after each kick, at 120 bpm: round 1 −2.3 / −2.0 dB (no dip: ducked throughout) → +9.6 / +9.9 dB (12 dB), +15.5 / +15.8 dB (18 dB). test_throw_hold: at most −0.56 dB above halfway, and −2.4 dB under the same drums without ducking.
- In freeze (no longer the default), turning up from silence gives silence: the bed holds what was in the tank when DECAY passed ~0.95.
- The M6 Ringing grid at CLEAN / DRIVEN DECAY 1 is now Hold territory: see the M6 note in `docs/m8-tuning-backlog.md` "Throw and Hold".
- The Plugin's `getTailLengthSeconds()` still says 10 s; a held tail is longer. Left as is (hosts only use it to keep processing after the input stops; Ableton keeps a reverb running anyway). Noted, not changed.
- Costs: see ADR 0039 for throw + round 1.
