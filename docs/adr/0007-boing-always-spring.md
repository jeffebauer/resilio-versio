# 0007 — BOING stays recognisably spring across its whole range

**Status:** Accepted, 27 Sep 2026

**Decision:** BOING fully CCW gives a soft, washy Spring: Chirps smeared but a faint boing still present. It never becomes a smooth, generic reverb. Fully CW gives an exaggerated Chirp.

**Why:** Spring ≠ room reverb (SPEC §2.2). Users want the spring colour. A generic-reverb end of the knob would be a dead zone for this module's purpose.

**Implication:** BOING's minimum keeps a floor on dispersion (allpass coefficient `a` and stage count M never reach "no dispersion"). Acceptance check: at BOING 0, the click render still shows a descending chirp in a spectrogram.
