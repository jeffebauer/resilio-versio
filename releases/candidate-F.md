THIS IS A CANDIDATE: "Resilio Versio F"

This build is called "Resilio Versio F" so it installs NEXT TO the version you already have ("Resilio Versio", from 1 Oct). I'd love you to compare the two: put each on its own return track, send the same snare or skank to both, and switch between them.

What changed since the 1 Oct build:
- The tank is modelled on my Teaching Machines Wellspring: gentler highs from the very first moment (like a real tank's coil and magnet), repeats that darken instead of staying bright and metallic, less boom in the low end, and a wider, blurrier stereo tail with no left-right flicker.
- TONE: right of noon is now King Tubby's "Big Knob", a steep low cut that thins the sound before it hits the springs, with a nasal ring on snares and rimshots. Fully left is still warm and dark.
- SPLASH: much bigger in its last quarter, and it works the same with DRIVE all the way down.
- SPRINGS 3 (right): the three springs now share energy, so hits bloom into a wash instead of dripping.

What I'm most curious about:
- Which one sounds more like a real spring tank to you, and which would you rather use?
- Does F sound too dull or too distant anywhere?
- Sweep TONE on a snare: does the level jump anywhere?

No Versio firmware with this one: it hasn't been tested on the module yet.
